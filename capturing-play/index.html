About the module

Two assignments, three stories

Capturing Play asked for photographs as narrative: first an open theme built around the idea of play, then a short documentary story about a person, a profession or a place. I chose a cricket team that keeps losing its grounds for the first, and for the second, the artists who become gods in Theyyam and the conductor on a bus.

Use the Composition switch at the top to see the same photographs hung four different ways.

Objective 1 · Changing Grounds

Research and shoot on a selected theme with the idea of capturing play: games people play in different locations and situations, theatre, or any theme that documents "play". Every image to be aesthetically captured, with around 40 final photographs.

Objective 2 · Theyyam and Bus Conductor

Document and tell a story in approximately 20 photographs about a person, a profession or a place.
